# Ugmuls 5.5e Automation v1.1.0

A cleaner chat experience and updated class automation for Player’s Handbook 3.0.0.

## New chat cards

- **Attacks, saves and damage stay together.** Roll results, damage, effects and Apply/Undo controls now share one grouped card, reducing chat clutter.
- **Healing gets the same treatment.** Healing and temporary HP rolls stay with their targets and application controls.
- **Multiple attacks are easier to follow.** Eldritch Blast beams, Scorching Ray attacks and Flurry of Blows strikes keep their individual targets and attack numbers.
- **Reactions are easier to find.** Compact feature icons sit together near the Apply controls. Hover to see what each does; Flash of Genius also names the creature receiving the bonus.
- **Follow-up healing is close at hand.** Healing Chalice and Improved Potent Spellcasting have small controls beside Apply/Undo.
- **Roll details stay open.** Expanded dice breakdowns remain open when a card refreshes.
- **Choose your layout.** Grouped attack, save and healing cards can be configured in Module Options.

## Player’s Handbook 3.0.0 updates

Class automation now connects to the updated book’s features, including:

- **Barbarian:** Brutal Strike applies its updated effects and shows the chosen blows as tags with hover descriptions. Reckless Attack uses the book’s Advantage benefit while retaining the module’s activation assistance.
- **Bard:** Bardic Inspiration rest recovery uses the book’s updated recovery rules.
- **Cleric:** Updated Warding Flare temporary HP, Channel Divinity and Sear Undead, Divine Strike, and Improved Potent Spellcasting healing.
- **Druid:** Updated Starry Form actions and Chalice healing, Primal Strike, Wrath of the Sea, Stormborn and Oceanic Gift. Circle of the Land keeps its convenient Long Rest selection, updating your prepared spells and Nature’s Ward resistance together.
- **Monk:** Updated Deflect Attacks/Energy, Flurry of Blows, Empowered Strikes, and Mercy’s Hand of Harm/Healing, including Poisoned application and free Harm during eligible Flurries.
- **Ranger:** Updated Dreadful Strikes and Dread Ambusher damage upgrades, shared uses and Mass Fear.
- **Sorcerer:** Elemental Affinity, Innate Sorcery’s attack Advantage and Trance of Order’s minimum rolls use the book’s built-in benefits.
- **Wizard:** Overchannel correctly tracks its free first use and escalating backlash with the updated feature.

Benefits now supplied by the book—such as Potent Spellcasting damage, Disciple of Life healing and level-based rest recovery—use those built-in rules instead of adding the module’s old version on top.

## Clearer module options

Class options now have short, plain-language descriptions explaining how each automation helps.

## Requirements and updating

- Requires **Foundry VTT 14** and **D&D5e 6.1.0 exactly**. Earlier D&D5e versions are incompatible.
- **Automated Conditions 5e** and **Dice So Nice!** must be installed and enabled.
- Use the official **Player’s Handbook (2024) 3.0.0** for its updated class features. Other books are needed only for the features you use.
- **Updating the book does not update features already on your characters.** Review and replace older owned features as needed. This release does not automatically migrate characters, and some retired automations now depend on the updated book items.
- Refresh all GM and player clients after updating. Start fresh casts and rolls to use the updated controls.
- Keep an active GM connected for shared damage, healing, resource and effect automation.
