# Starry Form

Activate Starry Form to choose Archer, Chalice, or Dragon. Confirm uses the native Wild Shape pool and Bonus Action. Cancel spends nothing.

- Archer posts a private attack action to owners and GMs, free as part of activation. Later turn-start cards use the normal Bonus Action. Select exactly one target in native range.
- Chalice adds native scaled bonus healing once per turn after a healing spell actually consumes a spell slot. It uses the sole eligible healed recipient automatically; multiple recipients open a one-target choice within 30 feet. The bonus retains the original spell group and its own healing target.
- Dragon copies the native base or level-10 Twinkling effect.
- Full of Stars, when owned, contributes its native resistances in every constellation.
- Twinkling Constellations posts a private start-of-turn selector. Changing once preserves the existing effect duration and spends no Wild Shape. Taking an action closes that turn's selection window.

The world setting is Stars Druid: Starry Form. Player mutations use active-GM validation. Effects end on native expiry or Incapacitated; removing a feature updates the active benefits. Native source effects are left intact.

Automated coverage uses installed source-item fixtures for scaling/effects, ownership, turn limits, rollback, slot gating and grouped healing. Foundry GM/player behavior still needs in-game confirmation.
