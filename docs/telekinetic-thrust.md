# Telekinetic Thrust

Click Psionic Strike on an applied weapon-damage card. Telekinetic Adept owners see a Psionic Strike popup with three vertical buttons: Damage Only, Prone and Push. Closing the popup cancels without spending a die. Fighters without the feature proceed with damage only.

The chosen mode is recorded with the strike. When Force damage is rolled, Prone or Push automatically prompts the native Strength save without another button or resource cost. Prone applies on failure through normal effect automation. Push movement of up to 10 feet horizontally remains manual. Save creation does not wait for Force damage application or animation; normal save-result and effect-animation safeguards remain active.
