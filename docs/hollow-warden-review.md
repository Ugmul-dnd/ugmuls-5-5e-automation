# Hollow Warden review

Ravenloft: The Horrors Within, reviewed October 27, 2026.

- Wrath of the Wild: native Transform Self, Favored Enemy consumption and Ancient Armor are preserved. Detect the actual applied Ghastly Form enchantment on the owned feature, using book source/activity identity rather than its blank identifier or the Barbarian's similarly named feature.
- Unnerving Aura: linked 10-foot grid-shaped token emanation, Region-layer visibility, enemies only (opposite disposition; exclude self, allies, neutral, defeated and ineligible tokens). Prompt the native Wisdom save on transformation and at the Ranger's turn start. Movement and entry do not trigger saves. Standard save prompting, result visibility, effect application, animations and Undo remain in use.
- Frightened ends at the start of this Ranger's next turn, including affected creatures that have moved out of the area. The region is removed when the native transformation ends/expires; incapacity suppresses the aura. Activation and turn markers prevent duplicate prompts across hooks/reloads. Native token attachment handles movement without rebuilding geometry every step.
- Hungering Might: automatically post and roll native self-healing after an attack hit while the Ranger is transformed and at 1–half effective maximum HP. Once per turn (including off-turn hits), shared with manual native activity use. Capture eligibility on the attack to avoid retroactive healing; respect native healing application, Dice So Nice and Undo. No transformation resources are spent again. The numeric HP check works without a Bloodied status icon; the healing assistant can run independently of the aura setting.
- Only the basic Frightened aura effect is automated, at every level. Menacing Aura's no-healing/no-Reaction additions, Strangling Roots, Ominous Strikes, Persistent Wrath and Prowling Retribution are deferred.
