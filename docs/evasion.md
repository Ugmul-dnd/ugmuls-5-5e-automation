# Evasion

Enable Evasion Assistance under Common automation options. It applies to any actor owning an Evasion feature, including Rogues, Monks and NPCs; no class or level assumption is made.

For native save activities requiring a Dexterity save and normally dealing half damage on success, each recorded target with Evasion gets no damage on success or half on failure. The damage selector is forced to 0 or ½ while eligible. Pending outcomes remain unchanged, and save-ability choices require an actual recorded Dexterity roll. Other abilities, full-damage/no-damage-on-success activities, attack damage and healing are unaffected.

Incapacitated actors do not benefit (including the native Unconscious, Stunned, Paralyzed, Petrified and Dead statuses). Native resistance, vulnerability, immunity and rounding apply after the correct save multiplier, without halving an already halved save result again. Evasion spends no resource or Reaction.

Works with manual and automatic damage application and grouped cards. Applied damage remains locked; Undo first to make changed eligibility or save results effective before reapplication. Original rolls are retained. Other modules that independently automate Evasion should not also apply an extra reduction. No movement hooks or actor/effect flags are added.
