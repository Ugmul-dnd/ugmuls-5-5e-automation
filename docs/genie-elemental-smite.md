# Oath of the Noble Genies: Elemental Smite

Enable **Genie Elemental Smite Assistance** under Paladin settings. The actor needs the native Faerûn **Elemental Smite** feature and the Paladin **Channel Divinity** resource.

After Divine Smite damage, its chat card offers four choices. This works with standalone Divine Smite and Divine Smite selected in weapon damage configuration. Only one choice can be used per cast. The Paladin’s owner or GM clicks it; one Channel Divinity use is spent. Canceling the choice or native use refunds it. GM arbitration prevents simultaneous GM/player choices. No movement hooks or chat-history scans are added.

- **Dao’s Crush:** applies the native Grappled + Restrained effect immediately, with no initial save/check. At the victim’s turn start, an optional action escape card offers Strength (Athletics) or Dexterity (Acrobatics) against the recorded Paladin spell save DC. Success ends both conditions. The creature-effect library entry can be customized or disabled. A connected GM applies unowned target effects through the existing effect/Undo workflow.
- **Djinni’s Escape:** opens the native self-buff card. Its effect is an applied temporary effect with physical resistance and Grappled/Prone/Restrained immunity until the end of the Paladin’s next turn, using the native source-end expiry. Automatic buff application follows the world setting; otherwise use the native effect tray. Move the token to the chosen teleport destination manually.
- **Efreeti’s Fury:** choose another visible enemy within 30 feet of the Paladin. The native 2d4 Fire damage card records both the Divine Smite target and the second creature. The damage configuration opens automatically with Normal and Critical options, recommending Critical when the original hit was critical. It resolves through its own pending damage card and retains both targets on manual retry.
- **Marid’s Surge:** choose additional visible creatures within 10 feet of the Paladin; the Divine Smite target is always included. The native Strength save uses the Paladin’s spell DC and applies Prone on failure through the normal save/effect automation. Move failed targets 15 feet manually.

Book/owned item data is preserved; temporary activity data is stored with its chat card. The level-15 Elemental Rebuke and level-20 Noble Scion reaction are separate features and remain manual.

Fury’s secondary-target list includes only opposing non-neutral dispositions within visible range. Hover a row to highlight its token; click its radio button to target it. Targeting a listed enemy on the map selects its row. With no eligible secondary enemy, damage proceeds against only the original target.
