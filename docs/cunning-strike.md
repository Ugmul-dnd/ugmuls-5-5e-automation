# Cunning Strike

Enable Sneak Attack Assistance and Cunning Strike Assistance under Rogue options. On a confirmed single-target weapon hit, select Sneak Attack, then Poison, Trip or Withdraw. Options cost one native Sneak Attack die each, removed before critical scaling; weapon dice remain unchanged. No choice is the default. All Rogue subclasses with the base feature are supported. Improved Cunning Strike offers two distinct choices and pays both costs.

Poison requires an owned Poisoner’s Kit tool. Trip requires a Large-or-smaller target. Their native saves use Dexterity-based Rogue DC and native Poisoned/Prone effects under existing application settings. Follow-up saves wait until damage is applied, including manual Apply. The Poison effect has an end-of-turn repeat Constitution save that removes it on success. Withdraw’s reminder appears in the damage flavor; move up to half Speed without opportunity attacks manually.

Canceling or previewing spends no dice/use. Existing Sneak Attack once-per-turn tracking remains. Damage Undo/reapply does not duplicate follow-up cards; use the save card’s own effect Undo to reverse its conditions. An active GM handles follow-ups affecting other actors. Subclass-specific extensions (such as Envenom Weapons, Supreme Sneak and Versatile Trickster) remain manual.

Sneak Attack defaults to Yes on Advantage or, for a finesse/ranged weapon without effective Disadvantage, when another conscious allied creature is within 5 feet of the recorded enemy. The check respects scene distance units, elevation and large-token edges; neutral/unknown allegiances remain manual. Manually selecting Sneak Attack immediately unlocks Cunning Strike; turning it off relocks the choices. No token-movement hooks are added.

## Devious Strikes

Owning the 2024 Devious Strikes feature adds Daze (2 dice, Constitution), Obscure (3 dice, Dexterity), and Knock Out (6 dice, Constitution) to the same selectors. Improved Cunning Strike allows two different options across both features; their costs are combined before critical scaling. Insufficient Sneak Attack dice block submission.

Daze applies the native reminder effect; choosing move/action/Bonus Action remains manual. Daze and Obscure expire at the end of the target’s next turn using native targetEnd expiry. Knock Out applies native Unconscious, repeats a Constitution save at each target turn end and removes that originating effect on success. Positive damage also removes tracked Knock Out effects after animations, preserving unrelated Unconscious effects; healing, zero damage and Undo do not wake the target. Native one-minute duration remains. These effects follow existing automatic/manual saved-effect settings and require an active GM for managed operations.
