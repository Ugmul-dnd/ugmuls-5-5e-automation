# Protective Field

Enabled under Fighter automation. Before applying incoming damage, click the Protective Field button for the Fighter and recipient. The recipient must be the Fighter or a creature visible within 30 feet. Spend one Psi Warrior Energy Die and the tracked Reaction; roll the native die plus Intelligence, with a minimum reduction of one.

Only the chosen recipient’s damage is reduced. Shared damage dice and other targets remain unchanged. Flat reduction follows native damage-type order, before native multipliers/resistance/vulnerability; it never becomes healing. HP application pauses while Protective Field resolves. Undo HP application before using it on an already-applied card. The committed reduction persists across HP Undo/reapply without spending another die.

Existing automatic-damage settings still control application; use manual application when you want time to consider reactions. No movement-driven refreshes are added.
