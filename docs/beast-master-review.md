# Beast Master review

Status: Complete for the current automation pass. Reviewed October 27, 2026 against the installed 2024 Player's Handbook. Bestial Fury bonus damage added; companion commands remain manual.

- Primal Companion: use native summon, Command and Restore Companion activities. Companion movement, Bonus Action commands, attack replacement and attacks remain manual.
- Exceptional Training: use the native Train Companion enchantment for Force damage selection. Additional companion actions remain manual.
- Bestial Fury: handle the two attacks manually. Automatically add the companion's bonus against its owning Ranger's live Hunter's Mark target in attack damage configuration. Resolve the native summon origin, use the Ranger's d6/d10, preserve the native noncritical bonus, and track the companion's use separately once per combat turn. Canceled damage does not spend the use. Outside combat there is no turn limit.
- Share Spells: optional sharing and range adjudication remain manual.

These are deliberate scope decisions, not unfinished implementation tasks.
