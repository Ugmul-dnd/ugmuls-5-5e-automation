# Psionic Strike

Enabled by default under Fighter automation. After applying weapon damage from a confirmed hit within 30 feet, click Psionic Strike on that damage card. Telekinetic Adept owners first choose Damage Only, Prone or Push in a vertical popup; closing cancels without spending. The selected save follows automatically alongside the Force damage roll, without a separate Thrust button. It spends one Psi Warrior Energy Die and immediately rolls the native die plus Intelligence as a separate Force damage card. It does not double on a critical hit. The original weapon roll and HP application are unchanged.

During combat, this is available once on the Fighter’s own turn. The target must have actually lost HP or temporary HP from the weapon damage; zero damage and undone applications do not qualify. GM and owning-player clicks use the active GM for resource arbitration. The Force damage uses normal resistance, automatic/manual application and Undo handling. Failed card/roll creation refunds the die and releases the turn marker.

There is no Psionic Strike choice in the damage configuration. Soulknife resources are not used. Outside combat, a qualifying applied weapon hit costs one die without a turn marker. Telekinetic Thrust remains outside this change.
