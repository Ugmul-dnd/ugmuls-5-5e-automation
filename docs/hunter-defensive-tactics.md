# Hunter Defensive Tactics

The native Short/Long Rest dialog includes a Ranger Options box with Hunter's Prey and Defensive Tactics choices for owned features. Current choices are preselected; pending choices survive Hit Dice rerenders. Completing the rest applies the choices without chat selection cards; canceling leaves the effects unchanged. The owner or GM can also use the 2024 Defensive Tactics feature to post a manual selector. Choose Escape the Horde or Multiattack Defense; one persistent self effect records the choice. Reusing a card switches that effect, without action/resource cost. Disabling it pauses the benefit.

- Escape the Horde follows the table rule: any attack against the Ranger during that Ranger's own combat turn counts as an Opportunity Attack and recommends Disadvantage. No manual attack classification or selector is required. Other turns and out-of-combat attacks do not receive this benefit automatically.
- Multiattack Defense automatically records confirmed hits. Further attacks by that creature against this Ranger have Disadvantage through the rest of the current combat turn. Other attackers and other targets are unaffected. The temporary hit markers expire at turn change/combat end; the persistent choice remains. Correcting the triggering hit to a miss removes its marker. Outside combat, this automation does not infer turn boundaries.

Both integrate AC5e recommendations and native attack configuration, retaining ordinary Advantage/Disadvantage cancellation. No additional attack, save, damage, or resource use is introduced.

Test both GM and player: complete a rest and switch choices; hit the Multiattack Ranger, attack again, attack another target, and advance the turn. For Escape, compare attacks against the Ranger during their own turn and another creature's turn. Confirm a disabled choice does not grant its benefit.
