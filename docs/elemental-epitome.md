# Elemental Epitome

Requires the owned 2024 Elemental Epitome and active Elemental Attunement. The assistance setting is under Monk.

- **Damage Resistance:** activating Elemental Attunement automatically posts the native resistance choice card. Use Damage Resistance again whenever you want to change it. Owners and GMs can click one resistance to replace the previous Epitome choice. Other resistance sources remain untouched. It ends with Attunement; no enforced turn-start switching restriction. Lightning and Thunder use the current resistance field.
- **Destructive Stride:** Step of the Wind creates a 5-foot grid-shaped emanation attached to the Monk, visible on the Region layer, and grants +20 Speed. Entry events add enemies to one shared damage card per activation turn. Roll the native Martial Arts die once and choose its elemental type; no save, extra Focus, or critical damage. Later targets share that roll, retaining existing application/Undo history. Remove unwanted recipients before applying damage. Caster and allies are excluded by default. Placement alone does not deal damage. The aura and speed expire at the end of the activation turn, or when Attunement ends. Outside combat they expire after six seconds of world time. Keep an active GM connected for Region creation and cleanup.
- **Empowered Strikes:** default-No on confirmed Unarmed, Empowered, and Elemental Strike damage. Add the native Martial Arts die in the selected strike damage type. Once on the Monk's own turn; off-turn attacks excluded. Preview/canceled rolls do not spend the turn use. Preserve the native critical-disabled bonus die, damage application/Undo, and existing rider save order. No extra Focus cost.

## Table verification

1. On the player client, activate Attunement and use Damage Resistance. Select Cold, then Lightning. Confirm one Epitome resistance; unrelated resistance remains.
2. Use Step of the Wind. Check +20 Speed and an attached 5-foot Region. Move past two enemies and one ally: enemies appear on the same damage card; ally and Monk do not. Re-entering an enemy must not add a second damage instance. Try another enemy after rolling damage too.
3. End the Monk's turn: Region and +20 Speed disappear. Ending Attunement removes resistance too.
4. On a confirmed Unarmed/Empowered/Elemental Strike hit, select Empowered Strikes. Check one extra native Martial Arts die matching the selected damage type, a locked second use, and availability next own turn. Canceling the first roll leaves it available. Confirm Push/Pull, Grapple, and Stunning still work together.
