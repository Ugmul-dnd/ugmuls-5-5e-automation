# Assassinate (2024 Assassin)

Enable Assassinate Assistance under Rogue options. The native feature and its damage activity are required. Its transferred Initiative Advantage effect stays unchanged.

In the first combat round, attacks against a combatant who has not yet taken a turn get target-specific Advantage through AC5e, with normal Disadvantage cancellation. Without AC5e, single-target native attack prompts receive the recommendation. Combatants currently taking a turn count as having started it.

First-round Sneak Attack hits automatically add the feature’s native Rogue-level damage formula to the initial damage roll, matching the weapon’s selected type. This fixed bonus does not double on a critical; Sneak Attack dice still follow their normal critical rules and Cunning Strike costs. The bonus does not require the target to be unacted, only a qualifying first-round Sneak Attack hit. No charge or additional action is consumed.

Fresh attack cards record round-one context, so completing linked damage after the round changes retains the original attack’s qualification. Cancellation/previews spend nothing. Roll corrections and Apply/Undo keep the original dice and shared damage flow.

Turn observation is local and uses current initiative order when first initialized after reload. Subsequent initiative reordering retains observed turns in that session. Unusual skipped/reordered turns before reloading may need table adjudication. Targets not in the tracked combat and ambiguous actor-only multi-token references are left manual. There are no movement hooks, historical chat scans or actor/Combat flag writes for turn observation. See the separate Envenom Weapons and Death Strike guides for their save/damage follow-ups.
