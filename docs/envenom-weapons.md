# Envenom Weapons

Enable Envenom Weapons Assistance under Rogue options. An actor owning the 2024 feature gains its extra Poison damage when using Cunning Strike Poison. No additional Sneak Attack dice are spent beyond Poison’s existing cost.

The initial and end-turn repeat Poison save cards retain the native Constitution save, recorded Rogue DC and normal Poisoned effect handling. On failure, the same card offers damage and the normal automatic damage prompt routes to a connected caster owner, with GM fallback. Pending and successful saves do not automatically prompt damage. The prompt setting remains in control; the native card’s Damage button and pending-card retries remain available for manual rolls/cancellation. Repeat-save success still removes the originating Poison effect.

Damage uses the Envenom activity’s native parts on a temporary copy. The official text specifies 2d6 but the installed default activity contains 2d8; generated helpers correct that known default to 2d6. Custom formulas and other deliberate edits are retained. Owned items and compendium content remain unchanged. The damage is not critical damage.

Native damage application ignores Poison resistance for these extra Poison rolls only. Poison immunity, vulnerabilities and other damage handling remain native; the original weapon damage and unrelated Poison damage are unaffected. Automatic/manual application, Apply/Undo and save adjustment locks use the existing shared workflow.

Reload clients and apply fresh Poison effects when testing newly installed helpers. An active GM is required for the existing managed follow-ups.
