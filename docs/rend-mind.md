# Rend Mind

Enabled under Rogue automation. A confirmed Psychic Blade hit with Sneak Attack offers Rend Mind on its completed damage card. Click to use the free use first or spend three Soulknife Psionic Energy Dice. The native Wisdom save applies Stunned for one minute on failure. The creature-effect library prompts its save at each target turn end and removes the effect on success.

The free use recovers on a Long Rest. Each attack can activate Rend Mind once. Cancelling or failing to create its save card refunds the cost. Both GM and owning players can request the feature; the active GM manages resource changes. Existing save/effect application options and Undo remain available. Psychic Teleportation remains manual.
