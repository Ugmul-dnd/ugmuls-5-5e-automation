# Elemental Rebuke

Enable **Elemental Rebuke Assistance** in Paladin settings. Requires the native level-15 Oath of the Noble Genies feature with uses remaining.

After an attack hits the Paladin, the attack card offers **Elemental Rebuke** to the owner and GM. Damage cards also expose it while damage is unapplied. A compact dropdown offers Acid, Cold, Fire, Lightning or Thunder; confirm with Use Rebuke or cancel for free. A player’s choice is validated and processed by the active GM. After the attacker’s save, the elemental damage prompt opens for the connected owner/GM who clicked Rebuke; if they disconnect, the active GM receives it.

The feature spends one native use and, for a tracked combat participant, marks the Reaction as used. The attacker receives the native Dexterity save against the Paladin’s spell DC, followed by 2d10 + Charisma damage of the chosen type, half on success. Existing save routing, damage prompting, application settings and animations remain in charge.

The original attack records the reduction per Paladin token. The native target multiplier is set visibly to ½, with normal rounding and resistance/immunity handling. Clicking ½ again does not halve it twice. The GM can explicitly override the multiplier. Other recipients are unchanged, and damage Undo/reapply retains the reduction without spending another use. Use the reaction before automatic damage applies; if damage is already applied, Undo it first.

The feature does not refund its spent use or Reaction when damage is undone. If creating the counterattack card fails after protection commits, protection and spending remain; resolve the native counterattack manually without consuming another use. No movement hooks or history scans are added.
