# Reanimator: Life Transfer

Use Life Transfer from Refined Reanimation or its native chat card. The action asks exactly one Yes/No confirmation: “Are you sure, this can not be undone”. No/close changes nothing. The acting user must own the caster and have a living, natively linked Reanimated Companion on the current scene. If multiple living linked companions are present, target the intended companion first; the workflow does not add a second selection dialog.

After Yes, the active GM revalidates ownership, companion identity and the caster's Reaction. Restore the companion's current HP to the caster, capped at the caster's effective maximum; do not transfer temporary HP. Reduce the companion to 0 HP and mark it dead. The native Life Transfer activity card records the result without a healing roll or HP Undo control. A source card that already reserved this Reaction can finish using the same reservation.

The GM finds eligible companions even when their private NPC data is unavailable to the player. The invoking client automatically centers native Death Burst on the sacrificed companion, without a placement cursor. Native Region creation and area-target confirmation still run. For players, the GM returns a temporary caster-owned copy with the companion's prepared damage formula and save DC; ownership of the NPC is unnecessary. The area still originates from the companion token. Preserve modifications, Improved Reanimation, damage typing and resistance behavior. Death Burst's ordinary save/damage workflow remains native. Canceling target confirmation does not reverse the committed Life Transfer; use the companion's native Death Burst to retry if needed.

The existing native Life Transfer card displays a visible summary: the companion sacrificed, HP supplied, HP actually restored and the caster's before/after HP. This distinguishes the full companion HP from capped healing. The summary supports standalone and grouped cards, respects content visibility and adds no second result card or HP Undo control.

Actor HP changes and completion metadata are GM-authoritative. Per-request receipts prevent duplicate healing. A failure before sacrifice attempts to restore the caster's unchanged HP and refunds only a newly reserved Reaction; concurrent HP changes are not overwritten. Life Jolt, automatic damage-triggered reactions and other Refined abilities remain deferred.
