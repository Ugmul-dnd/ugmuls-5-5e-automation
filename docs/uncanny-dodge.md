# Uncanny Dodge

Enable Uncanny Dodge Assistance under Rogue options. A Rogue owning the native feature and its Reaction activity gets a button only on confirmed incoming unapplied damage cards, including grouped cards. Melee, ranged and spell attack rolls qualify; saving-throw-only damage does not.

Click Uncanny Dodge before applying damage. Clicking confirms that the Rogue can see the attacker; visibility adjudication remains at the table. The connected owner or GM can click; the active GM validates ownership and Reaction availability. Incapacitated actors cannot react. The feature has no charge pool. Combat Reaction tracking uses the existing tracker and resets normally. Outside combat, or with tracking disabled, spending the Reaction remains table-managed.

Only that Rogue’s recorded target receives the native half multiplier. Original damage dice remain unchanged; resistance, vulnerability, immunity and rounding stay with native damage application. Clicking Half again does not quarter damage. Explicit multiplier controls remain available. Undo applied damage first if it is already locked; reapplication preserves the recorded Dodge without spending another Reaction. Canceling/no click spends nothing.

No movement refresh hooks are added. Resource/effect/turn refreshes use the shared chat queue. Disable overlapping Uncanny Dodge automation in other tools to avoid duplicate reductions.
